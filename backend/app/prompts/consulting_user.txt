CONSULTING REQUEST

BUSINESS PROBLEM / USER QUESTION:

{question}

RESEARCH EVIDENCE:

{context}

SOURCE INFORMATION:

{sources}

ANALYSIS REQUIREMENTS:

Analyze the business problem using the evidence provided by Cognexa.

Your response must be grounded in the available evidence. Do not invent facts, metrics, market figures, company information, sources, or assumptions that are not supported by the evidence.

Clearly distinguish between:

* Evidence-backed facts
* Analytical interpretation
* Assumptions
* Strategic recommendations

CONSULTING ANALYSIS:

When relevant, explain:

1. Problem Definition

   * What is the actual business problem?
   * What appears to be driving the problem?

2. Root Cause Analysis

   * Identify potential root causes supported by the evidence.
   * Clearly indicate when a root cause is only a hypothesis.

3. Current Situation

   * Summarize the most important evidence.
   * Identify important KPIs, trends, comparisons, or business signals.

4. Strategic Options

   * Present realistic options available to the organization.
   * Explain the advantages and disadvantages of each option.

5. Benefits and Trade-offs

   * Explain expected benefits.
   * Identify important trade-offs and resource considerations.

6. Risks

   * Identify evidence-supported business risks.
   * Mention uncertainties and limitations in the available evidence.

7. Recommended Approach

   * Recommend the most appropriate approach based on the available evidence.
   * Explain why the recommendation is preferable.

8. Implementation Plan

   * Provide practical implementation steps.
   * Where useful, organize actions into immediate, medium-term, and long-term phases.

9. Success Measurement

   * Identify KPIs or measurable outcomes that could be used to evaluate success.
   * Do not invent target values unless sufficient evidence exists.

10. Dashboard Opportunities

* When sufficient structured data exists, identify useful KPIs, charts, tables, trends, or geographic visualizations that Cognexa could present in a business dashboard.

11. Presentation Opportunities

* Identify major sections or findings that could be included in a professional Cognexa presentation or report.

EVIDENCE QUALITY:

If sources disagree, explicitly identify the conflict instead of silently selecting a value.

If the available evidence is insufficient, clearly identify:

* What information is missing
* Why it matters
* What additional evidence would be useful

Do not fabricate missing information.

OUTPUT STYLE:

Provide a professional consulting response that is:

* Evidence-driven
* Practical
* Structured
* Objective
* Business-oriented
* Clear and actionable

Avoid unnecessary filler, generic consulting language, unsupported claims, and false precision.

The objective is to transform:

Question → Evidence → Analysis → Insight → Recommendation → Action

into a practical decision-support response for the user.
